02 // Why AtlasVanguard

What you actually get

No paywalls, no tryout gauntlet. Just a working roster and people who show up.

◆

Squads for every rank

Casual, ranked and competitive divisions — you get placed by rank and how hard you want to sweat, not by how loud you are in chat.

◉

Organised game nights

Posted schedules in Discord, pinged roles, and an actual start time. If a night is announced, a squad is forming.

▲

Coaching that doesn't sting

VOD reviews, aim routines and role walkthroughs from our veteran players. Feedback is direct, never personal.

✦

Clan wars & scrims

We play other clans across Valorant, CS2 and CODM, and run in-house tournaments with bragging rights on the line.

⬢

Zero-tolerance for toxicity

Rage at the game, never at the squad. Our mod team acts on reports the same day — it's why the voice chats stay usable.

✚

More than one game

Burned out on ranked? Switch lobbies without leaving the clan. Your friends and your Discord stay the same.

03 // Recruitment

How to join

The whole process happens in Discord. Nobody gets asked for money, passwords or account access — ever.

01

Click the invite

Join the AtlasVanguard Discord with the button on this page. One click, no forms.

02

Read #rules, pick your roles

Grab the game roles for whatever you play so the right squads can tag you for game nights.

03

Post an intro in #applications

In-game name, region, rank and what you're looking for. An officer replies, usually within a day.

04

Queue with us

Play a couple of games with your squad. If it clicks, you're in the roster — permanently.

04 // The standard

What we expect

Four lines. That's the whole rulebook.

  • Be 13+ and act like it. No harassment, no slurs, no drama bait — in voice, text or DMs.
  • Show up or say so. Real life comes first, but a "can't make it tonight" goes a long way.
  • Play your role. Communicate, don't tilt your squad, and take the loss with the win.
  • No cheating, no account sharing. Instant removal, no appeal, no exceptions.
